NEON Excimers Instructions

Goal:
The goal of the game is to last as long as possible against waves of oncoming enemies. Every 10 enemies you kill grant you a level up, which lets you choose 1 of 4 random upgrades. The threshold for leveling up increases by 2 for each successive level. Reach the highest level you can, and flex on your friends!

Controls:
WASD - Movement
Left Click - Shoot
Space - Dash
Esc - Pause
Tab - Stats Window

Good luck and have fun!
